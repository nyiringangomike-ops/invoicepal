Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. InvoicePal runs entirely in your browser. Your invoices are stored locally on your device.

How do I get paid?

Send the PDF by email, or embed your payment link (PayPal, Stripe, Wise) in the invoice notes. We focus on making the invoice itself perfect.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. All data stays in your browser's local storage. Export a JSON backup anytime from the settings.

How does the Pro plan work?

Pay once via Stripe. You're instantly unlocked in the app on the confirmation page. Single-click upgrade, cancel anytime.
